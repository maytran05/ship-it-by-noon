# My app

**Domain:** Campus study spots

**User story:** As a student, I need to find study spots filtered by noise level and whether they have power outlets, so that I can pick a place where I can focus and keep my laptop charged.

**What the user types or picks:** A noise level (silent, quiet, or lively) from a dropdown, and a checkbox for "needs outlets". The page then lists matching study spots with their building, floor, and hours.
